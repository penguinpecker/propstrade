1. DECISIONS SO FAR (planning, 2026-09-21)
------------------------------------------
- Chain: Solana. Money: USDC only. Venue: GMTrade (gmtrade.xyz) = the GMX-DAO-authorised Solana
  deployment of GMX v2 (ex GMX-Solana). Code: github.com/gmsol-labs/gmx-solana. Name: Props.trade.
- Model:
    challenge fee (USDC) -> simulated challenge (offchain) -> funded account.
    Funded account = real GMTrade positions opened from a Props vault program. Trader can trade,
    cannot withdraw capital. Drawdown limit (e.g. 5%) closes/caps losses.
    GMTrade's GM pools are the counterparty and pay winners. Props' vault only posts margin.
    Profit split 80% trader / 20% vault.
- Why the vault must NOT take the opposite side of trades: in a prop firm the trader only pays a
  fee. If the vault also hedges, trader losses net to zero and trader wins become pure cost
  (it just moves the loss from "when they lose" to "when they win", plus double fees).
- Vault P&L = challenge fees + 20% of funded profits - drawdown losses.
  Worked example from planning: $10k account, 5% drawdown -> $500 real risk. If ~1 in 10 pass at a
  $100 fee, that is ~$1k of fees per funded trader. (Pass rate is an industry guess, not data.)
- Start lean: seed the vault with own money ($5k-$20k), no outside LP deposits at first
  (outside deposits start to look like a pooled investment), small accounts first, cap funded slots
  to vault size, waitlist the rest. Open LP deposits only after real pass/payout data.
- Checkout can be chain-agnostic later; the margin vault must be on the venue's chain (Solana).
- Build the vault so the venue is swappable (GMTrade first; a deeper venue later for big accounts).
- Things that kill a prop firm: hedge farming (two accounts, long + short), gaming simulated fills
  (latency arbitrage vs the price feed), real winners draining the vault, oversized positions,
  regulation (geo-block the US).
- Idea from planning: GMX's Builder Program pays integrators a cut. On GMTrade, builder fees are
  NOT live (unreleased code, cap 0) and would be circular for Props anyway (see section 6).

What the vault MUST do (design rules):
- ONE signer PDA per funded account, e.g. seeds ["funded", vault_state, account_id]. It must hold NO
  data and be system-owned (GMTrade uses the owner as rent payer and as 'from' of system
  transfers; a data-carrying account cannot be 'from'). Keep vault state in a separate PDA.
  Keep its lamports at >= 890,880 (zero-data rent floor) or exactly 0.
  Positions are keyed by (store, owner, market, collateral token, side): one shared owner would
  MERGE different traders' positions.
- Fund each signer PDA with ~0.05 SOL. Costs (approx): user account 0.0045 SOL once; position
  ~0.0056 rent + ~0.0225 liquidation reserve; each order ~0.0245 up front (order rent 0.0181,
  escrow token accounts, execution fee >= 0.0003). Most is refunded when orders/positions close.
- Pre-create the PDA's token accounts (USDC + every allowed market's long/short token). If the
  receiver lacks an ATA for an output token, the keeper's close is SKIPPED and funds + ~0.02 SOL rent
  sit in escrow until the owner closes the order. 30 such stuck program-owned orders exist on
  mainnet today (wSOL dust). Build a "close finished order" step (Flipper and Imperial both did).
- owner = receiver = the signer PDA, set by OUR program. Never pass trader-chosen receiver, source
  account, referrer or builder-fee accounts through. Receiver is fixed at creation and cannot be
  changed later (states/order.rs:740-773). Refunds, liquidation/ADL proceeds and claimable rebates
  always go to the OWNER.
- Nobody but the owner can move the owner's funds out through GMTrade. Keeper/unsigned-owner
  instructions only pay TO the owner. So "trader can't withdraw" depends only on our program.
- Payouts are not always USDC: profit on longs arrives in the market's long token (e.g. wSOL)
  unless decrease_position_swap_type = PnlTokenToCollateralToken and that swap succeeds; with
  should_unwrap_native_token it arrives as plain SOL. For liquidations the KEEPER picks that flag.
  The sweep step must handle USDC, wSOL and SOL. (Pure USDC-USDC markets pay in USDC.)
- Two-step orders: our program creates; only ORDER_KEEPER (8 addresses; 7 plain keys + 1 multisig
  vault) executes, observed 4-8 s later, execute + close in one tx. Market orders expire 30 min
  after last update (store request_expiration = 1800 s; lazy: applied when a keeper retries).
  min_position_age_for_manual_close = 0.
- NO fill notifications: GMTrade callbacks only accept an allowlist = GMTrade's competition program
  2AxuNr6euZPKQbTwNsLBjzFTZFAevA85F4PW9m9Dv8pc (programs/callback/src/interface.rs:9-26; locked down
  after Zenith Jun-2025 C-1). We need our own sync step (read Position account + token balances)
  run by a bot / permissionless crank.
- Native TP/SL exist (LimitDecrease, StopLossDecrease), executed by GMTrade keepers, updatable,
  no expiry. BUT: not guaranteed ("trigger orders are market orders and are not guaranteed to
  execute at the trigger price" - docs); cancelled (not "frozen" - the docs are wrong for Solana)
  on non-temporary failure; cannot close an insolvent position (user decreases run with
  is_insolvent_close_allowed = false); stay open after a manual close and would hit the trader's
  NEXT position in that market/side; keepers can cancel them once the position account is gone.
  No program-owned native trigger order exists on mainnet today to copy.
- CLOSED MARKETS (big): while a market is flagged Closed (stocks/ETFs outside US hours, forex at
  weekends), GMTrade refuses to CREATE or EXECUTE decrease orders (states/market/mod.rs:337-344;
  allow_closed only for collateral-only MarketIncrease, order.rs:361-367). Only liquidation runs,
  and prices are frozen at the close, so the real risk is the gap at reopen. Neither our bot nor a
  stop-loss can close stock/forex trades outside hours.
- HARD LOSS CAP that needs no bot: GMTrade positions carry no debt to the owner, so a position can
  never lose more than the collateral posted. If the vault posts only the trader's REMAINING loss
  budget as collateral, the vault can never lose more than that, even if a close is late, the
  market is closed, or liquidation is slow/disabled. Liquidation only ends the position.
  Trade-offs: "$10k account" becomes buying power on leverage (e.g. $500 collateral on $10k = 20x);
  account-wide limits across several positions still need our own accounting.
- Anchor side: vault uses crate gmsol-programs = 0.10.0 (declare_program!(gmsol_store); CPI via
  gmsol_programs::gmsol_store::cpi), anchor-lang 0.31.x ONLY (^0.31.1 excludes 0.32 and 1.x).
  GMTrade builds with Anchor 0.31.1 / Solana 2.1.21. Keep the vault instruction top-level or 1 CPI
  deep (GMTrade self-CPIs for events + ATA program -> depth).
- Devnet: store program + same store address + 34 markets exist, but GMTrade's devnet USDC
  (UsdsBvJe6sPKBzZhEo9bDDVg6d3695gJonz9eVSAUpP) has no mint authority, no devnet order fills were
  seen recently, and the devnet build differs (test-only feature). Plan real tests on mainnet with
  a few dollars.
- GMTrade can change the rules: program upgradeable by the 4-of-7 multisig with a 10-minute delay;
  FEATURE_KEEPER can disable order creation/execution/liquidation per kind.

5. TRUST / "DECENTRALISED" CLAIMS (verified)
-------------------------------------------
- Don't say "decentralised". Honest, strong: "funded accounts are real GMTrade positions held by
  our Solana program; every funded trade and payout is public."
- The strongest safety claim is structural: "the vault can never lose more than it puts into a
  trade" (collateral = loss budget design). Say "designed to" until built and tested.
- "Trader can't take the capital out" holds only if our program pins every order's receiver to the
  vault-controlled PDA and never exposes withdrawals. "We can't change the rules" holds only once our
  upgrade key is locked or behind a timelocked multisig.
- Anyone-can-call "check and close" instruction in the vault is possible (verifiable rule), but
  GMTrade's keepers still execute closes and the price input is the weak point: GMTrade's onchain
  PriceFeed accounts are stale (updated only when orders execute; SOL 109-398 s old, ETH ~6 h);
  Chainlink Data Streams onchain verification needs an allowlisted, paid account; Pyth sponsored
  feeds move only every 55 s or 0.5% (at 10x that is 5% of equity = the whole limit).
- The challenge stays offchain. Best you can do: publish every challenge trade + commit a hash
  onchain (tamper-evident, not proof of fair fills). Small real-money challenges would send losers'
  money to GMTrade's pool, which breaks the fee model.
- Competitors (primary sources, 2026-09-23):
    Propr (propr.xyz; Hyperliquid + Polymarket + Lighter; SwissBorg-backed; Propr Limited, BVI,
      unregulated): "All Propr accounts, including 'Funded Accounts', are 100% simulated." It copies
      ("A-books") some signals at its discretion. Still says "All execution is transparent and
      verifiable on-chain". Hiring a "Software Engineer - Solana" (Rust/Anchor).
    Carrot Funding: challenge params in an NFT, A/B booking, 50% of fees to a rethink.finance vault,
      Hyperliquid execution, "24h Guaranteed Payout", integrating Oasis ROFL. Deploys some real capital.
    Hyro Protocol (HyroTrader): Solana vault prop protocol, rules fixed at vault creation, devnet,
      Ackee audit, mainnet target Oct 2026 (their claims). Closest to our design.
- Wording to avoid: decentralised, guaranteed, yield, investment, "we pay you". Others call payouts
  "rewards" and say most traders fail. Outside LP deposits = pooled-investment risk. (Not legal
  advice.) CFTC v. My Forex Funds was dismissed over the regulator's own misconduct; it does not
  make the model legal.
- Hedge risk we cannot see onchain: a funded trader hedging on another venue gets a free option paid
  by the vault; losing on purpose into a thin pool they LP in. Rules + market allowlist + identity.
